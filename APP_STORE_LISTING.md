# TaskLock - App Store Listing

## Name
TaskLock - ADHD App Blocker

## Subtitle
Lock distracting apps. Do work.

## Description
TaskLock is the ultimate productivity tool designed specifically for people with ADHD or anyone struggling with phone distraction. 

Instead of relying on willpower to stay off TikTok, Instagram, or games, TaskLock uses Apple's Screen Time API to completely block your distracting apps until you finish your real-world tasks. 

**HOW IT WORKS:**
1. Pick the apps that distract you the most.
2. Add the tasks or daily routines you need to get done today.
3. Lock it in. Your distracting apps will instantly become inaccessible.
4. Finish your tasks. Once you check them off in TaskLock, your apps are automatically unlocked.

**FEATURES:**
• **Bulletproof Blocking:** Built on Apple's official Screen Time API. No VPNs, no sketchy workarounds. 
• **Daily Routines & Habits:** Build powerful streaks for your morning and evening routines.
• **Barcode Scanning (Optional):** Force yourself out of bed by requiring a barcode scan (like your toothpaste) to unlock your apps.
• **Strict Mode:** Prevent yourself from deleting tasks or editing timers to cheat the system.

Take control of your dopamine, build better habits, and unlock your focus.
